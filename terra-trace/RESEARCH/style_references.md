# Style References — Amati · Tiru · Modifikasi

A method for learning from other channels without copying them.

| Step | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Amati** (observe) | What exactly does the reference do, second by second? | An observation sheet (below) |
| **Tiru** (adopt) | Which of its mechanics are genre conventions we can use too? | A list of adopted mechanics |
| **Modifikasi** (make it ours) | What do we change so the result is unmistakably TERRA TRACE? | Brand rules and style choices |

**What can be adopted:** structure, pacing, hook patterns, how text sits on image, the timing of reveals. In short, the grammar of the genre.

**What is never taken:** footage, screenshots, music, script wording, titles, thumbnails, logos, a colour and font combination that identifies the other channel, or a shot-for-shot remake.

## Proposed TERRA TRACE signatures (modifikasi)

These are proposals waiting on the style decision.

1. **Evidence tag.** Every satellite shot shows PLATFORM · SENSOR · DATE plus its credit. Attribution becomes part of the look instead of an afterthought.
2. **The trace line.** A single amber line traces the feature the narration is talking about. It is the only accent colour, and it moves with the story.
3. **Claim-ladder narration.** "The image shows… / According to… / Scientists agree… / One explanation is…" The audience learns to trust the wording.
4. **The same-season rule.** Comparisons are honest, and the video says so.
5. **Sign-off.** A closing line that lands on the tagline (e.g. "…written on Earth."), if it doesn't become repetitive.
