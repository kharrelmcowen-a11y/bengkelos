## Timing budget

The structure is fixed; the word counts are targets. They assume a natural English documentary pace of about 2.2 words per second. To calibrate yourself, record one test read and divide its words by its seconds.

| Beat | Time | Job | EN words |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Hook | 0:00–0:03 | One image and one line that raise the question | 6–8 |
| 2. Geographic discovery | 0:03–0:12 | Where we are, what we're looking at, how big it is | 16–22 |
| 3. Satellite evidence and investigation | 0:12–0:32 | Show the evidence: observations and measurements (rungs 1–2) | 36–48 |
| 4. Explanation and context | 0:32–0:44 | Why it happened, with sources (rungs 3–4); hypotheses labelled (rung 5) | 20–28 |
| 5. Conclusion | 0:44–0:55 | What it means now, and one line to remember | 16–24 |
| End card (optional) | 0:55–0:60 | Wordmark, or a loop back to the hook; no narration | 0 |
| **Total** | **40–60 s** | | **90–130** |

**Indonesian:** keep the same beats and the same seconds. Indonesian usually needs fewer words for the same time, so time your read rather than matching the English word count.
