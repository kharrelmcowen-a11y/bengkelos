# Style References — Amati · Tiru · Modifikasi

A method for learning from other channels without copying them.

| Step | Question | Output |
|---|---|---|
| **Amati** (observe) | What exactly does the reference do, second by second? | An observation sheet |
| **Tiru** (adopt) | Which of its mechanics are genre conventions we can use too? | A list of adopted mechanics |
| **Modifikasi** (make it ours) | What do we change so the result is unmistakably Topographica? | Brand rules and style choices |

**What can be adopted:** structure, pacing, hook patterns, how text sits on image, the timing of reveals. In short, the grammar of the genre.

**What is never taken:** footage, screenshots, music, script wording, titles, thumbnails, logos, characters or illustrations, a colour and font combination that identifies the other channel, or a shot-for-shot remake.

## REF-001 — observation sheet

**Video:** "Kenapa Kalimantan Nggak Punya Harimau, Padahal Bali Pernah Punya? #Shorts" ("Why doesn't Kalimantan have tigers, when Bali once did?"). 3.1M views on 2026-11-22.

**Channel:** Sejengkal Peta. 14.8K subscribers and 30 videos on 2026-11-22. Its own description: *"geografi dijelaskan lewat peta skala asli … Video pendek tiap hari dalam Bahasa Indonesia"* ("geography explained through true-scale maps … a short video every day, in Indonesian").

### What was seen (cover plus frames at about 25 %, 50 % and 75 %)

| Frame | Visual | On-screen text | Graphics |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cover / ~50 % | Top-down satellite basemap; Borneo filled with a flat olive tint; dark-blue ocean | "PENELITI MENDUGA" ("researchers suspect"), white, all caps, top centre | Five cartoon tiger-face icons scattered over the island |
| ~25 % | Satellite basemap of mainland Southeast Asia down to Bali | "THAILAND KE BALI / TANPA PASPOR" ("Thailand to Bali, no passport"): line 1 white, line 2 yellow | A dotted route line along the land; a full-body cartoon tiger; a passport icon crossed out |
| ~75 % | Satellite basemap; Sumatra filled with an orange tint | "TINGGAL HARIMAU SUMATRA / ± 39 EKOR" ("only the Sumatran tiger left, ± 39"): line 1 white, line 2 yellow | Tiger-face icons on the island |

### The same pattern across three other big hits

These are 1aWyrHiGrrg (1.8M views), X_t46KijU0s (1.7M) and -diVSonqLXM (1.3M).

- **Satellite basemaps with a region tinted.** There is a pulsing ring to mark a spot (Ujung Kulon) and an animated wave front crossing the sea.
- **Flat cartoon cut-away scenes** on a sky-blue background: a smiling wave carrying luggage on a highway ("OMBAK MUDIK — TOL TANPA LAMPU MERAH": the wave's holiday trip home, a toll road with no red lights), and a field-researcher character in a pith helmet beside a cross-section of a gas well.
- **An end card that asks for comments**: "TULIS DI KOMENTAR — KAMU PILIH MANA?" ("write in the comments: which do you pick?") with two or three illustrated options, or "JAWAB YA ATAU NGGAK" ("answer yes or no").
- **Two-line headline at the top** in every frame: a white setup line, then a punch line in yellow or red. Heavy rounded sans-serif, all caps, dark stroke.
- A faint channel watermark at the top.
- **The cover is a frame from the video** with its headline, not a separately designed thumbnail.

### Title formula (from the 28 Shorts listed)

- **"Kenapa … ?" plus a parenthetical twist** is the dominant form: *Kenapa Badak Jawa Cuma Tersisa di Ujung Kulon? (Mau Dipindah Gara-gara Tsunami)*.
- **Myth vs. science:** *(Nyi Roro Kidul vs Sains)* got 1.7M views; *(Mitos vs Sains)* got 127K.
- **"Kalau …, … jadi gimana?"** is the what-if form.
- **Casual spoken Indonesian:** *nggak*, *gimana*, *kayak*, *bikin*.
- **Indonesia-centric topics**, especially wildlife, disasters, myths and local landmarks. The four biggest hits are all about Indonesian places.

### Not observed

Narration (human or synthetic), pacing, music, caption style during speech, the transitions, and the exact length.

## Tiru — what Topographica adopts

| Mechanic in REF-001 | Why it works | How we use it |
|---|---|---|
| A "Why…?" title that the video answers | It promises a single, clear payoff | Already our title rule. EN: "Why…?" plus an optional twist in brackets. ID: "Kenapa…?" |
| Two-line headline: setup, then punch in an accent colour | It reads in one glance with the sound off | Line 1 in Paper, line 2 in Trace amber; claims on rung 3 or above only |
| A tinted region over the satellite map | The viewer instantly sees *which* place | Added to the graphics grammar as a "region fill", on context maps only: accurate boundaries, from cited boundary data |
| A pulsing ring to mark a spot | It finds the point fast on a small screen | A variant of our pin: one pulse, then a static pin with coordinates |
| A route line showing movement | It tells a "how it got there" story | Use it only when the route is sourced, and label it if approximate |
| Myth vs. science framing | Curiosity plus a payoff | Hidden Earth episodes: the myth named once, the evidence carries the video |
| A question at the end that invites comments | It drives comments | We ask about evidence: "Which year would you trace next?" or "What else changed between these two images?" |
| Local cultural metaphors (*mudik*) | Instant familiarity for Indonesian viewers | The ID version localizes metaphors as well as words |

## Modifikasi — where Topographica deliberately differs

| REF-001 | Topographica |
|---|---|
| The satellite map is a backdrop | **The satellite image is the evidence.** It is real and dated, and it carries an evidence tag (platform · sensor · date · credit) |
| Cartoon characters and icons are the signature | No characters or mascots. Only monoline pictograms, and only on context maps |
| One undated basemap per scene | Before/after pairs from the same season, with both dates visible |
| Indonesian only, Indonesia-centric | Global, English first, with an Indonesian localization |
| Casual and playful throughout | EN restrained and investigative; ID relaxed but smart, with local metaphors but no mockery and no inflated claims |
| Numbers shown on screen without a visible source | Every number on screen is rung 2 or 3, and the source is named |
| Daily posting | Two a week to start, with checklists (CONTENT_STRATEGY §6) |

## Strategy notes for later

- REF-001's biggest hits are Indonesian places told to Indonesian viewers. For the ID channel, an Indonesia-relevant topic such as TP-001 (Kalimantan) may matter more than it does for EN. Test this once the ID accounts exist.
- The channel reached millions of views with 30 videos, so the format, not the archive size, did the work. A few outliers came from strong "Kenapa…?" questions, which is worth tracking in our own performance log.

## Genre conventions (general, not from REF-001)

These were listed before REF-001 was observed and come from general familiarity with satellite Shorts. REF-001 showed none of them in the frames seen.

| Convention | Decision |
|---|---|
| A zoom from orbit to the target at the start | At most 2 s if used; real imagery with attribution only |
| Dramatic music and impact hits | Rejected: an ambient bed, and no hits under unverified claims |
| Imagery with no date or source | Rejected: the evidence tag on every shot |
